Update 10 — Undying
🔱 Spear fishing
• The Fish tab now has Fishing and Spear tabs. In Spear, fish roam around: tap one for an osu-style circle minigame.
• 20 buyable spears with time, extra misses, luck, value and a few perks.
• The 5 best spears have a chance (1% to 8%) for the new Crafty mutation (x750).
• New shop rod: Gleefin Rod (✦ 1q) with the Oppressed mutation (x1,111.11).
• Two spear-only raid bosses: Gigalith and Monstrous Cusk.
🐟 Fish
• 3 new fish in Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythical.
📖 Index
• New index rewards for completing sets.
🎣 Masters Rod
• Now only needs every buyable rod.
🎣 New Rod Shop rods
• Tidebreaker Rod (✦ 100M): 2,000% luck, 70% green zone, 1.6x control, 25% Omniscient mutation, passive 30x XP.
• Abyssal Sovereign Rod (✦ 4B): 4,000% luck, 72% green zone, 1.7x control, 20% Infinity mutation, +50% fish value.
• Undying Champion (✦ 1T): 10,000% luck, 75% green zone, 1.3x control and a 30% chance for the new Revival mutation (×888). Ability: every second a timing bar appears. Hit Shift (or the ⚡ Strike button on mobile) while the marker is in the gold zone for +50% progress.
• Bangsplit Rod (✦ 1T): 6,666% luck, 66% green zone, 1.1x control, passive 50x XP and a 10% chance to give 100x XP to your catch.
🔄 Rebirth
• 15 more rebirths (Rebirth 16 to 30, up to ×50,000 credits and ×300,000 XP). Rebirth 30 needs level 100,000.
• New rod rewards: Reborn Rod (Rebirth 10), Eternal Phoenix Rod (Rebirth 25) and Rod of the Final Cycle (Rebirth 30). Each has a periodic chance to boost your progress.
⭐ Levels
• Max level raised from 30,000 to 100,000. Rewards every 1,000 levels up to 50,000, then every 10,000.
• Levels after 30,000 need much more XP, and the climb gets steeper the higher you go.
• Level 50,000 reward: 1 Infinity-mutated God of Olympus.
• Level 75,000 reward: enchants can now roll up to tier XX. Until then, enchants stop at tier X (was IV). Higher tiers give much bigger bonuses and are much rarer.
• Level 100,000 reward: the dark red and dark orange Cataclysm skin, 100 lucky aura rolls and 1 Cataclysmic-mutated fish (×9,999) of your choice, with a base value of up to ✦ 100,000.
✨ Enchanting
• New Auto roll: choose an enchant and a minimum level, and it rolls your relics until a roll fits or you run out.
🎟 Codes
• Code BigHugeUpdate: 100 aura rolls, 100 lucky aura rolls, 1,000 enchant relics and 1 hour of every time token boost.
🧬 Mutations
• New rod mutation: Revival (×888). New reward mutation: Cataclysmic (×9,999).
🔢 Compact numbers
• With Compact numbers on, 1,000T now shows as 1q, 1,000q as 1qt and 1,000qt as 1sx.
🤖 Auto fish
• New Auto fish option under the Fish button, unlocked once for 60 ⏳ time tokens. It casts and reels for you, skips boss raids, and only runs while the game is open and visible.
Update 9 — Olympus
🌋 Secret bosses
• Every normal raid boss now has a 5% chance to turn into a secret version: Molten Scylla 🌋, Fallen Dark Leviathan 🌑 and Felled Nessie 🦴. They have the same difficulty but are worth 20x more (base value ✦ 300,000 / ✦ 600,000 / ✦ 1,200,000). They show up in the Index.
🪽 Mega Bosses
• The Godly tab is now the Mega Bosses tab. Godly bosses live in its Godly section, next to the new Olympus section.
🔱 Olympus
• Two new mega bosses: Olympian Devourer 🔱 (base value ✦ 10,000,000, 1,000,000 base XP) and God of Olympus ⚡ (base value ✦ 25,000,000, 2,500,000 base XP).
• While fighting them your green zone is capped at 30% and your control at 1.1x.
• Each challenge needs an Olympus Key 🗝️, which every catch has a 7.5% chance to give. The key is used up win or lose.
🪱 Baits
• New Baits card in the Fish tab. Each bait makes a group of fish easier to hook: Worm (x5 commons), Shrimp (x5), Shiny Lure (x5), Squid Chunk (x5), Golden Bait (x8 legendaries) and Mythic Bait (x10 mythicals). Buy packs of 10 with credits, equip one, and one is used each time a fish bites.
🎟 Codes
• Code OlympianTrials: 50 aura rolls, 25 lucky aura rolls, 50 enchant relics and 1 Olympus Key.
🧙 Wizzard King’s Rod nerf
• Passive XP is now 20x (was 50x) and the duplicate chance is 10% (was 20%).
🪱 Baits
• The Baits card is now collapsible.
🌳 Skill tree
• Every skill now has 100 levels (was 10), with the same bonus per level. Levels 11 to 100 each cost about 12% more than the last. Your existing levels carry over.
🔄 Rebirth
• 10 more rebirths (Rebirth 6 to 15, up to ×600 credits and ×2,200 XP). Rebirth 15 needs level 30,000.
⭐ Levels
• Max level raised from 15,000 to 30,000, with rewards every 1,000 levels.
• Level 20,000 reward: 1 Infinity-mutated secret raid boss of your choice.
• Level 30,000 reward: the Infinitive aura (+7,777% luck, x77 XP, +77% green zone, +777% fish value, 7.77% chance for every fish to have the Influx mutation, x777 value).
Update 8 — Leaderboards
🏆 Ranks
• New Ranks tab with four leaderboards: most money (peak credits), most XP earned, time played and rarest catch.
• Rarest catch compares how unlikely a fish and its mutation are to catch.
• Only players with accounts appear. Stats update when your game syncs.
Update 7 — Online accounts
👤 Accounts
• Create an account with a username and password (no email needed). Your progress now saves online and follows you to any device.
• When creating one you can start fresh, bring over the progress saved on this device, or import an old save code.
• Guest play still works, and save codes still work in Settings.
• Log out or sync manually in Settings. There is no password reset, so remember your password.
Update 6 — Dinosaurs
⭐ Levels
• Leveling is easier: XP needed is now 1.8x the original (was 2.5x).
🦖 Dinosaurs
• New Dinos tab with 8 eggs: Basic 10K, Weird 50K, Rare 150K, Epic 500K, Legendary 2M, Mythical 10M, Otherworldly 50M and Godly 1B.
• Every egg holds 5 dinosaurs, each with a different buff (luck, XP, money, mutation odds or green zone).
• Equip up to 3. While reeling, a 25% chance every second that a dinosaur bites the bar for +25% progress.
• Dino mutations: Fossil (2x buffs), Triassic (4x) and Blessed (8x).
• Green zone is now capped at 95%.
Update 5 — Time tokens
⏳ Time tokens
• You earn 1 token every minute (time away counts for up to 60 minutes).
• Tap your credits at the top to open the Time token shop.
• Boosts: 2x XP, 2x Luck, 2x Money and 2x Mutation chance, 10 minutes each for 20 tokens.
• Four limited skins rotate weekly, 150 tokens each.
Update 4 — A steadier pace
⭐ Levels
• Leveling needs more XP (2.5x at first, eased to 1.8x in Update 6).
• New level rewards every 5 levels up to 100.
• An XP bar now sits under the top bar.
🔥 Streaks
• Catch fish in a row without losing one. Every 10 gives credits, every 25 a relic, every 50 an aura roll. Losing a fish resets it.
🛒 Rods & rebirth
• Shop rods cost a bit more.
• Rebirth bonuses are gentler: x4/x6, x6/x10, x9/x16, x14/x28, x20/x45 (credits/XP).

📋 Quests

• All three Godly bosses unlock the Godly mutation and have the 5% Godlike Rod drop.
🪽 Godly Chardbys
• Boss ability: every second it has a 30% chance to freeze your bar for half a second.
• New natural mutation: Godly (×150, 0.02% base). It only unlocks after you catch the Godly Chardbys.

While fighting it your green zone is capped at 40% (smaller green zones are left alone), your rod abilities are half as effective, and every second it has a 30% chance to freeze your bar for 0.5s. Catching it unlocks the natural Godly mutation (×150) and has a 5% chance to drop the Godlike Rod (7,777% luck, 40% green zone, 0.5x control, 10% Godly mutation) (passives like luck and XP bonuses are unchanged).

🧬 Mutations

🐙 Boss raids
• Each bite now has a 7% chance to be a boss (was 1%).
🧬 Mutations
• 6 new natural mutations: Frozen (×4), Molten (×6.5), Radioactive (×11), Cursed (×14), Spectral (×30) and Error (×40.4).
🔄 Rebirth
• New Rebirth tab. Rebirth resets your level and credits; rods, auras, fish and everything else stay. Rebirth 1 (level 200 + ✦ 100,000): ×10 credits and ×20 XP. Rebirth 2 (level 500 + ✦ 1,000,000): ×15 credits and ×30 XP. Rebirth 3 (level 1,000 + ✦ 10,000,000): ×20 credits and ×50 XP. Each rebirth’s bonus replaces the last one.
• The first rebirth unlocks the Skill Tree: buy permanent upgrades (5 levels each) for credits, XP, luck and natural mutation chance. Skill upgrades never reset.
🏹 Hunter
• A hunter visits every 3 minutes and stays for 2 minutes. He asks for a specific fish with a specific natural mutation and pays 2x that fish’s base value with the mutation applied. Hunters follow the Villager requests setting.

• You can now close the Traveling Merchant with the ✕ button on his card. Closing him early forfeits that visit.
• The Rod Shop is sorted by how hard each rod is to get: Shop rods by price, Special rods from easiest to hardest.
🎯 Perfect catch
• Keep the fish inside the green zone from the moment it first enters the bar until you land it for a perfect catch: 5x XP.
⭐ Levels
• Levels no longer change the difficulty of the minigame.
• The XP curve is flatter at high levels: each level needs much less XP than before (level 10,000 now needs about 135K XP per level, down from 440K).

✨ Enchanting
• Enchants now have levels I–IV (60% / 27% / 10% / 3%). Higher levels give bigger bonuses. Enchants you already have count as level I.
